# Pantry Staples

Unless a recipe calls for something specific, assume these staples (what we stock, mostly from Costco):

- Salt: Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- Butter: Kerrygold salted butter
- Stock/broth: Better Than Bouillon (chicken and beef varieties on hand)
- Thai curry paste: Mae Ploy

Don't spell out the brand in ingredient lists — just write "butter", "salt", "chicken stock", etc. But write the method as if these specific staples are being used: account for the fact that the butter is salted (not unsalted), that Diamond Crystal is less salty by volume than table salt or Morton's kosher, and that Better Than Bouillon is itself well-salted. This affects things like how much salt to add and when, and whether a recipe's original seasoning steps (e.g. ones written for unsalted butter) need adjusting.

# Meal Prep

Meal-prep recipes are never pre-assembled. Prep components ahead (proteins cooked, sauces made, veggies shredded/chopped, each stored separately), then write the method so the meal is finished and assembled to order on the day it's eaten: reheat proteins, cook anything that's best fresh (e.g. bacon, eggs), warm breads/wraps, then assemble. Split the method into a prep-day section and a day-of section.
