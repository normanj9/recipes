# Arroz Rojo

_Adapted from the Arroz Rojo recipe in La Mesa Mexicana._

## Ingredients

### Rice
- 250g (1¼ cups) long-grain white rice
- 1 tablespoon lard
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 serranos

### Tomato Mixture
- 2 large roma (plum) tomatoes, roughly chopped
- ½ small white onion, roughly chopped
- 90g (⅓ cup) tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 10g BTB chicken paste

---

## Method

### 1. Soak and Rinse the Rice
1. Soak the rice in a bowl of cold water for 10 minutes.
2. Drain and rinse.

### 2. Make the Tomato Mixture
1. Place the tomato, onion, tomato paste, salt, and BTB in blender.
2. Blend until smooth. Add enough water to reach 2 cups total liquid.

### 3. Toast the Rice
1. Heat the lard in a large saucepan over medium–low heat.
2. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, for 1–2 minutes until lightly browned.
3. Remove the garlic cloves with a slotted spoon and discard.
4. Add the drained rice to the pan and stir for 8 minutes, or until lightly toasted.

### 4. Cook the Rice
1. Stir in the blended tomato mixture until combined.
2. Cover and bring to the boil.
3. Reduce the heat to low and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Score 5mm (¼in) long slits all over the chillies and add them to the pan.
5. Gently stir the rice, cover, and continue to cook for a further 3 minutes, adding more water if the mixture starts to look dry, until the liquid is completely absorbed and the rice is cooked through.

### 5. Serve
1. Rest with the lid on for a few minutes before serving.
2. Leftover rice will keep in an airtight container in the fridge for 3 days.
