# Jeera Pulao (Indian Rice Pilaf)

## Ingredients

### Rice
- 300g / 1½ cups basmati rice

### Liquid
- 350ml water
- 10g Better Than Bouillon paste
- 3 gelatin ice cubes

### Aromatics
- 1 medium yellow onion, halved and thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ tsp ground ginger

### Whole Spices
- 1½ tsp cumin seeds
- 4 green cardamom pods, cracked
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick (or ¼ tsp ground)

### Ground Spices & Finish
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ¼ tsp garam masala (finish only)
- ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 2 tbsp ghee or unsalted butter

---

## Method

### 1. Soak the Rice
Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs clear, then soak in cold water for 30 minutes. Drain well before using.

### 2. Make the Stock
Combine the water, Better Than Bouillon paste, and gelatin ice cubes in the pot over low heat. Stir until paste is dissolved and cubes are fully melted. Transfer to a container and keep warm, or make directly in a small saucepan alongside.

### 3. Fry the Onion
Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the sliced onion with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring occasionally, for 10–12 minutes until deep golden and beginning to crisp at the edges. Remove about a third of the onion and set aside on a paper towel — this is your garnish.

### 4. Bloom the Whole Spices
Reduce heat to medium. Add the cumin seeds, cardamom pods, cloves, and cinnamon directly to the onion remaining in the pot. Stir constantly for 60–90 seconds until the cumin is fragrant and starting to darken. Add the garlic and ground ginger and cook another 60 seconds.

### 5. Toast the Rice
Add the drained rice to the pot and stir to coat every grain in the spiced fat. Add the turmeric and ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt. Toast for 2 minutes, stirring gently.

### 6. Cook
Pour in the warm stock. Stir once, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover tightly with a lid (use foil under the lid if it's not snug) and cook for 12 minutes undisturbed.

### 7. Steam and Rest
Turn off the heat and let the pot sit covered for 10 minutes. Do not lift the lid.

### 8. Serve
Uncover, add the garam masala, and fluff gently with a fork. Taste for salt. Plate and top with the reserved crispy onion.
