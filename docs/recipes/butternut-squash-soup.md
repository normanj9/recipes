# Classic Butternut Squash Soup

_Adapted from the Classic Butternut Squash Soup recipe on Serious Eats._

## Ingredients

### Vegetables and Aromatics
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon (14g) unsalted butter
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped (about 8 ounces; 225g)
- 2 medium cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- 1 large butternut squash (about 3 pounds; 1.4kg), peeled, seeded, and cut into 1-inch chunkszz
- 1 large carrot, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 celery rib, cut into 1-inch chunks

### Liquids and Seasoning
- 4 cups (1L) homemade chicken stock, or store-bought low-sodium chicken broth
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

### Optional Garnishes
- 1/4 cup (60ml) heavy cream
- Toasted pumpkin seeds
- Croutons

---

## Method

### 1. Sauté Aromatics
1. Heat olive oil and butter in a large saucepan over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add onion and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until softened but not browned, about 4 minutes.
3. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.

### 2. Add Vegetables and Stock
1. Add squash, carrot, and celery. Stir to combine.
2. Add chicken stock and season with salt and pepper.

### 3. Simmer
1. Bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Reduce to a simmer and cook until squash is completely tender, about **20 minutes**.

### 4. Purée
1. Using a hand blender, purée the soup directly in the pot until completely smooth.
2. Alternatively, purée in batches using a countertop blender.

### 5. Adjust Consistency
1. If the soup is too thick, whisk in additional stock or water to adjust.
2. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

### 6. Serve
1. Ladle into bowls.
2. Garnish with cream, pumpkin seeds, or croutons if desired.
