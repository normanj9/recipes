# Chile Rojo Enchilada Sauce (Roux-Thickened, Small Batch)
_Adapted from the Enchilada Sauce derivative in the Chile Rojo Base recipe on Jeremy's Recipe Collection._

## Ingredients

### Base & Liquid
- 4 cubes (2 squares) Chile Rojo Base — about 2 tbsp / 32g
- 1 cup (237 mL) chicken stock

### Roux & Enrichment
- 1.5 tbsp (21g) lard
- 1.5 tbsp (12g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp (16g) tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp (1g) ground cumin
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Lime/ACV, to taste
---

## Method

### 1. Make the Roux
Melt the lard in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking constantly, 2-3 minutes, until pale gold and toasty-smelling.

### 2. Bloom the Paste and Tomato
Whisk in the Chile Rojo Base and tomato paste. Cook, stirring, 1-2 minutes until darkened and deeply toasted-smelling.

### 3. Add Cumin
Stir in the cumin and toast 30 seconds.

### 4. Whisk in Stock
Add the stock a splash at a time, whisking out lumps before adding more.

### 5. Simmer
Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook, whisking occasionally, 8-10 minutes, until it coats the back of a spoon and no longer tastes of raw flour.

### 6. Season
Add acid (start with 1/2 t) and taste for seasoning.

### 7. Serve
Use immediately over enchiladas, or cool and refrigerate up to a week.
