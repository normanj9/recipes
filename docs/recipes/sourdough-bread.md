# No-Knead Sourdough Bread

_Adapted from the Charlotte Rutledge recipe on KingArthurBaking.com._

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (227 g) ripe (fed) sourdough starter  
- 1 ¾ cups (397 g) lukewarm water  
- 5 cups (600 g) bread flour  
- 1 tablespoon (18 g) table salt  
- 2 teaspoons diastatic malt powder (optional, for color and stronger rise)  

---

## Method

### 1. Mix the Dough  
1. Weigh or gently spoon and level the flour.  
2. In a large bowl or bucket, combine all ingredients.  
3. Stir until a sticky, rough dough forms (30–60 sec with a mixer or by hand).

### 2. Bulk Fermentation  
4. Cover and let the dough rise for **1 hour**.  
5. Gently fold the dough over itself, cover, and let rise another **1 hour**.  
6. Repeat folding and rising once more (total **3 hours** of rise/fold cycles).  
7. Cover and refrigerate the dough **8–48 hours** to rest.

### 3. Shape & Final Rise  
8. Turn the dough onto a well-floured surface and shape into a ball.  
9. Cover and let rest **15 minutes**.  
10. Shape to fit your baking vessel (e.g., Dutch oven, loaf pan).

### 4. Bake  
11. Bake as directed (typically **55 min to 1 hr**; specifics may vary with oven and vessel).

### 5. Serve  
12. Cool slightly, slice, and enjoy warm or at room temperature.
