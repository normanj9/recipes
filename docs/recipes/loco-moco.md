# Loco Moco

**Serves:** 4  
**Total Time:** ~35 minutes  

---

## Ingredients
  
- 3 cups rice
- 4 burger patties

### Gravy
- 3 Tbsp unsalted butter (42 g)  
- 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour (24 g)  
- 2 cups beef stock (480 ml)  
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce  
- ½ tsp onion powder  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- Fresh black pepper to taste  

### To Serve
- 4 eggs, crispy fried

---

## Instructions

1. **Cook the Rice**
   Start rice in the cooker so it’s ready when the rest is done.  

2. **Make the Gravy**

   - Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat.  
   - Stir in flour; cook 2–3 minutes until golden and nutty.  
   - Gradually whisk in beef stock.  
   - Add soy, Worcestershire, onion powder, garlic powder, and pepper.  
   - Simmer until thick and glossy. Adjust seasoning.  

3. **Grill burgers**
    Pull at 150.

4. **Fry the Eggs**
   Cook crispy-fried.

5. **Assemble**
   In each bowl: rice → burger → ladle of gravy → fried egg.  
   Garnish with scallions and furikake or togarashi.
