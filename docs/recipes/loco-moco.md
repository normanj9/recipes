# Loco Moco

**Serves:** 4  
**Total Time:** ~35 minutes  

---

## Ingredients
  
- 3 cups rice
- 4 burger patties

### Gravy
- 3 Tbsp unsalted butter (42 g)  
- 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour (24 g)  
- 2 cups beef stock (480 ml)  
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce  
- ½ tsp onion powder  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- Fresh black pepper to taste  

### To Serve
- 4 eggs, crispy fried

---

## Instructions

1. **Cook the Rice**
   Start rice in the cooker so it’s ready when the rest is done.  

2. **Make the Gravy**

   1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat.  
   2. Stir in flour; cook 2–3 minutes until golden and nutty.  
   3. Gradually whisk in beef stock.  
   4. Add soy, Worcestershire, onion powder, garlic powder, and pepper.  
   5. Simmer until thick and glossy. Adjust seasoning.

3. **Grill burgers**
    Pull at 150.

4. **Fry the Eggs**
   Cook crispy-fried.

5. **Assemble**
   In each bowl: rice → burger → ladle of gravy → fried egg.  
   Garnish with scallions and furikake or togarashi.
