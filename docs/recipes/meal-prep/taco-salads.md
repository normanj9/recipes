# Taco Salad Meal Prep

_An original recipe, built for weekly lunch prep. The taco meat is seasoned with the [Chile Rojo Base](../sauces/chile-rojo-base.md)._

## Ingredients

### Protein
- 680g (1.5 lb) ground chicken
- 15g (1 tbsp) lard
- 120ml (1/2 cup) Bodhizafa IPA (Georgetown Brewing), for deglazing
- 60g (1/4 cup) [Chile Rojo Base](../sauces/chile-rojo-base.md), thawed (about 4 of the 2x2 squares)
- 16g (1 tbsp) tomato paste
- 7g (1 tbsp) ground cumin
- 2g (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- 5g (1¾ tsp) salt
- 15ml (1 tbsp) lime juice

### Beans & Veg
- 425g (15 oz) black beans, canned, drained and rinsed
- 300g (about 2 cups) fire-roasted corn, frozen
- 484g (1 large clamshell) baby spinach
- 300g (about 2 cups) diced tomato or pico de gallo
- 170g (6 oz) sliced black olives, canned, drained
- 1 small red onion (100g), thinly sliced
- 120ml (1/2 cup) white vinegar
- 60ml (1/4 cup) water
- 4g (1 tsp) sugar
- 5g (1 tsp) salt

### Dressing
- 340g (1.5 cups) plain Greek yogurt
- 45ml (3 tbsp) lime juice
- 1 tsp lime zest
- 15g (1/4 cup) chopped cilantro
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 3g (1/2 tsp) salt
- 30–60ml (2–4 tbsp) water, to thin

### Toppings & Crunch (add day-of)
- 115g (1 cup) cotija, crumbled
- 2 large avocados (about 400g), cubed
- 15g (1/4 cup) chopped cilantro
- 85g (about 2 cups) tortilla chips, roughly broken

---

## Method

### 1. Pickle the Onion
Combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a jar. Add sliced red onion, submerge, and let sit at least 30 minutes. Refrigerate separately from the rest — don't store it with the assembled containers.

### 2. Season and Cook the Chicken
Melt lard in a pan over medium-high. Add ground chicken and salt and break it up. Keep cooking until the juices it releases have boiled off and the meat starts to sizzle and brown, 8–10 minutes.

Push the meat to the edges and add the Chile Rojo Base, tomato paste, cumin, and smoked paprika to the cleared spot. Fry, stirring, 1–2 minutes, until the paste darkens and smells toasty, then stir it through the meat. Deglaze with the Bodhizafa, scraping up the fond, and reduce until the sauce is gone and just coats the meat. Finish with lime juice off heat.

The base already brings the chile, garlic, and oregano, so no chili powder, garlic powder, or dried oregano is needed. It's only lightly salted, so it barely changes how much salt the meat needs. The salt is kept a little under 1% of the meat's weight because the cotija and olives add plenty at the table.

### 3. Char the Corn
In a dry hot pan (carbon steel works well), cook frozen corn until it picks up char spots, 4–5 minutes.

### 4. Make the Dressing
Whisk yogurt, lime juice, lime zest, cilantro, garlic, jalapeño, and salt together. Thin with water to a pourable consistency. Store in a squeeze bottle.

### 5. Assemble Storage Containers
Divide spinach, chicken, black beans, corn, diced tomato, and black olives across containers.

### 6. Serve
Top each portion with dressing, avocado, cotija, pickled onion, and tortilla chips just before eating.
