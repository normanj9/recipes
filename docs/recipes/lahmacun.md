# Lahmacun (1.5 lb Ground Beef) — With Homemade Dough, Baked on a Sheet Pan

## Ingredients

### Dough (8–10 thin rounds)
- Warm water: **1 cup (240 g)**
- Instant yeast: **1.5 tsp (5 g)**
- Sugar: **1 tsp (4 g)**
- Bread/AP flour: **3 cups (360 g)**
- Salt: **1.5 tsp (9 g)**
- Olive oil: **1 tbsp (15 g)**

### Meat Topping (food-processor method, no crushed tomato)
- Ground beef: **1.5 lb (680 g)**
- Onion: **1 medium (~150 g)**
- Garlic: **3 cloves (~12 g)**
- Red bell pepper: **1 small (~120 g)**
- Flat-leaf parsley: **1 cup packed (~40 g)**
- Tomato paste: **3 tbsp (50 g)**
- Red pepper paste (optional): **1 tbsp (20 g)**
- Olive oil: **2 tbsp (30 g)**
- Paprika: **2 tsp (4 g)**
- Aleppo pepper: **2 tsp (4 g)**
- Cumin: **1 tsp (2 g)**
- Salt: **15 g**
- Black pepper: **1 tsp (2 g)**
- Water: **2–4 tbsp**, as needed for spreadability

### Garnish
- Lemon wedges  
- Onion–parsley–sumac salad  
- Pickled peppers (optional)

---

## Method

### 1. Make the Dough
1. Mix warm water, yeast, sugar. Rest **5 min**.  
2. Add flour, salt, olive oil. Knead **8–10 min** until smooth.  
3. Cover and rise **1 hour** until doubled.  
4. Divide into **8–10 balls**; rest **15 min**.

### 2. Make the Topping (Food Processor)
1. In the processor: onion chunks, garlic, bell pepper → pulse to a **fine mince**, not a puree.  
2. Add parsley → quick pulses to incorporate.  
3. Move veg mix to a bowl.  
4. Add ground beef, tomato paste, red pepper paste, olive oil, paprika, Aleppo, cumin, salt, pepper.  
5. Mix thoroughly. Add **2–4 tbsp water** to achieve a smooth, spreadable consistency.

### 3. Shape the Bases
1. Preheat oven to **475°F (246°C)**. Place your sheet pan inside to preheat **optional but recommended** for crisper bottoms.  
2. Roll each dough ball **very thin**—about **10–11 in (25–28 cm)** diameter. You want almost tortilla-thin.

### 4. Assemble
1. Lay parchment on the hot sheet pan (or use a room-temp sheet pan if easier).  
2. Place one rolled dough round on parchment.  
3. Spread a **thin, even layer** of topping, edge-to-edge. Press it in.

### 5. Bake
1. Bake **6–9 minutes** until edges blister and topping is cooked.  
2. Remove and repeat. Rotate pan if browning unevenly.

### 6. Serve
- Squeeze lemon.  
- Add onion–parsley–sumac salad.  
- Roll and eat immediately.
