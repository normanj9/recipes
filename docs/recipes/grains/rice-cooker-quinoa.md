# Rice Cooker Quinoa

_A quick weeknight side for two. Tested with 1 scoop; scaled to 2 scoops, since 1 was a little light for two people._

## Ingredients

- 2 rice-cooker scoops white quinoa
- Water to the 2 line
- 2 tsp chicken stock paste
- 1 bay leaf
- Grated parmesan, to finish (about ¼ cup, to taste)

---

## Method

### 1. Load the Rice Cooker
1. Rinse the quinoa well in a fine strainer and drain thoroughly.
2. Add the quinoa to the inner pot.
3. Stir the stock paste into a little warm water until dissolved, add it to the pot, then fill with water to the 2 line.
4. Lay the bay leaf on top.

### 2. Cook
1. Cook on the Quick setting.
2. When it finishes, pull the bay leaf.

### 3. Finish
1. Fluff the quinoa and stir in the parmesan.
2. Taste before adding any salt. The stock paste and parmesan usually season it enough.
