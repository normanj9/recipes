# Coffee Beans

_Portioning a 5 lb bag of whole-bean espresso into freezer bags, one bag per jar refill._

## Yield
- 8 bags of 283 g, 15 doses of 18 g each
- About 4 g left over from the 5 lb (2268 g) bag

## Equipment
- Scale (0.1 g resolution is ideal)
- Vacuum sealer and bags
- Marker or labels

---

## Method

### Bulk Prep

#### 1. Portion
Weigh out 8 bags of 283 g each from the 5 lb bag.

#### 2. Seal
Vacuum seal each bag. Label it with the roast date and "15 doses."

#### 3. Freeze
Lay the bags flat and freeze them right away.

### Refill

#### 4. Thaw
When the jar is nearly empty, pull one bag and leave it **sealed** until it reaches room temperature, a few hours or overnight. Opening it cold makes moisture condense on the beans.

#### 5. Transfer
Open the bag and pour all of it into the jar. Don't refreeze any of it.

#### 6. Dose
Weigh 18 g per shot. You'll have about 13 g extra at the end of each jar to absorb weighing error.
