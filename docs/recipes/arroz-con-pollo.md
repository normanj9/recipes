# Nicaraguan Arroz con Pollo

_Adapted from the Serious Eats recipe on SeriousEats.com._ :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken
- 1 whole large chicken (about 4–5 lb), cut into 8 pieces, skin removed  
- 1 medium yellow/white onion (≈225 g), peeled and cut into 6 wedges  
- 1 medium green bell pepper (≈130 g), seeded and quartered  
- 6 garlic cloves (≈30 g), peeled and smashed  
- 1 Tbsp (9 g) kosher salt (use half by volume if using table salt)  
- 2 tsp (6 g) whole black peppercorns  
- 1 lime, halved  

### For the Rice
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) olive oil  
- 1 medium yellow/white onion (≈225 g), finely chopped (≈1 cup)  
- 1 garlic clove (≈5 g), minced (≈1 tsp)  
- 2 tsp (6 g) kosher salt (again adjust if using table salt)  
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) tomato paste  
- 2 cups (425 g) long-grain white rice  
- 2 medium carrots (≈115 g), peeled and diced (≈1 cup)  
- 1 quart (945 ml) chicken broth  

### For the Arroz con Pollo
- 3 Tbsp (45 g) unsalted butter, divided  
- 8 oz (225 g) smoked sausage (like Lil’ Smokies or kielbasa), sliced ¼-inch thick  
- ¼ lb (115 g) deli ham, coarsely chopped  
- 1 medium yellow/white onion (≈225 g), finely chopped (≈1 cup)  
- ¾ cup (75 g) drained piquillo peppers, finely chopped  
- 2 Tbsp (30 g) tomato paste  
- 6 Tbsp (120 g) ketchup  
- ¾ cup (115 g) pimento-stuffed olives, sliced  
- ⅓ cup (35 g) capers, drained  
- 1½ cups (185 g) frozen peas  
- Kosher salt, to taste  
- 1 cup (≈60 g) finely grated Parmesan cheese (optional)  
- Buttered white toast (optional, for serving) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## Method

### 1. Poach the Chicken
1. Place the chicken pieces, onion wedges, bell pepper, smashed garlic, salt, and black peppercorns in a Dutch oven.  
2. Add enough cold water to cover chicken by ~2 inches.  
3. Squeeze lime juice over and add the juiced lime halves to the pot.  
4. Bring to a boil over medium heat, then reduce to medium-low and simmer until chicken is cooked through (~20 minutes).  
5. Transfer chicken to a large bowl; once cool, shred, discarding bones. Reserve 4 cups strained broth and set aside (save extra broth for another use). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

### 2. Cook the Rice
1. Wipe out the Dutch oven. Melt 2 Tbsp butter over medium heat, then add olive oil, finely chopped onion, minced garlic, and salt; cook until softened (~5 minutes).  
2. Stir in tomato paste until darkened (~1 minute).  
3. Add rice and carrots, stirring until the rice is thoroughly coated (~1 minute).  
4. Pour in reserved broth and bring to a boil. Continue until most liquid evaporates and small bubbles appear.  
5. Lower heat, cover, and cook until rice is tender (~20 minutes). Remove from heat, fluff with a fork, and set aside. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

### 3. Build the Arroz con Pollo
1. In a large skillet with high sides, melt 2 Tbsp butter over medium heat.  
2. Add sausage and ham; cook until lightly browned (~3 minutes). Transfer to a plate.  
3. Melt remaining 1 Tbsp butter in empty skillet. Add finely chopped onion and piquillo peppers; cook until softened (~5 minutes).  
4. Stir in tomato paste and cook until darkened (~1 minute).  
5. Add ketchup and shredded chicken, tossing to coat well.  
6. Stir in reserved rice, then the browned sausage and ham.  
7. Mix in olives, capers, and peas; cook, stirring occasionally, 10–15 minutes until flavors meld.  
8. Season with salt to taste. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

### 4. Serve
1. Serve warm topped with grated Parmesan if using.  
2. Accompany with buttered white toast if desired. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
