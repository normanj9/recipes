# Lemon Pepper Chicken Pasta with Roasted Broccoli

_Dinner for 2._

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 605 g / 1⅓ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 5)
- 9 g / 1 Tbsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 3 g / 1 tsp salt
- 14 g / 1 Tbsp neutral oil

### Roasted Broccoli
- 340 g / ¾ lb broccoli crowns, cut into bite-size florets
- 14 g / 1 Tbsp neutral oil
- 1.5 g / ½ tsp salt
- 1 g / ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

### Pasta
- 200 g / 7 oz short pasta (rigatoni, orecchiette, or penne)
- 14 g / 1½ Tbsp salt, for the pasta water

### Lemon Pepper Butter Sauce
- 42 g / 3 Tbsp butter
- 6 g / 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 4 g / 1½ tsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 120 g / ½ cup pasta water, plus more as needed
- 25 g / ⅓ cup finely grated parmesan
- 2 g / 1 tsp lemon zest (1 lemon)
- 30 g / 2 Tbsp lemon juice (1 lemon)

---

## Method

### 1. Heat the oven and water
Put a sheet pan in the oven and preheat to 500°F / 260°C. Let the pan heat fully. Bring 2 L / 8 cups water to a boil with the 14 g / 1½ Tbsp salt. That's lighter than usual for pasta water because the butter, lemon pepper, and parmesan all bring salt.

### 2. Season the chicken
Pat the thighs dry. Mix the 9 g / 1 Tbsp lemon pepper with the 3 g / 1 tsp salt and season both sides of the chicken. The blend already contains salt, so this stays lighter than a normal dry brine.

### 3. Sear the chicken
Heat the 14 g / 1 Tbsp oil in a large carbon steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Lay in the thighs smooth side down and cook undisturbed 5 to 6 minutes, until well browned. Flip and cook 4 to 5 minutes more, to 175°F / 80°C internal. Move to a cutting board to rest, and keep the skillet with its fond.

### 4. Roast the broccoli and cook the pasta
Toss the florets with the oil, salt, and pepper. Carefully spread them on the hot sheet pan in a single layer and roast about 12 minutes, until deeply browned at the edges. At the same time, drop the pasta and cook it 2 minutes short of the package time. Scoop out 240 g / 1 cup of pasta water before draining.

### 5. Make the sauce
While the pasta cooks, set the skillet over medium-low and add the butter. Once it foams, add the garlic and the 4 g / 1½ tsp lemon pepper, and cook 1 minute, scraping up the fond, until the garlic is fragrant but not browned. Add the 120 g / ½ cup pasta water and bring it to a simmer.

### 6. Finish the pasta
Slice the chicken into strips and add any resting juices to the skillet. Add the drained pasta and toss over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes, until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the pasta. Take the pan off the heat. Add the parmesan, lemon zest, and lemon juice and toss again, loosening with more pasta water a splash at a time if it tightens. Fold in the chicken and broccoli. Taste before adding any salt; it usually doesn't need more.

### 7. Serve
Divide between two bowls and finish with more freshly ground black pepper and parmesan.
