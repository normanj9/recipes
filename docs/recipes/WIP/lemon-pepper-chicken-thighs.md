# Lemon Pepper Chicken Thighs with Smashed Potatoes and Roasted Broccoli

_Dinner for 2._

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 605 g / 1⅓ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 5)
- 9 g / 1 Tbsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 3 g / 1 tsp salt
- 14 g / 1 Tbsp neutral oil

### Lemon Pepper Butter
- 28 g / 2 Tbsp butter
- 3 g / 1 tsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 15 g / 1 Tbsp lemon juice (½ lemon)

### Smashed Potatoes
- 454 g / 1 lb small Yukon Gold potatoes (golf-ball size)
- 19 g / 2 Tbsp salt, for the boiling water
- 28 g / 2 Tbsp neutral oil
- 1.5 g / ½ tsp salt
- 1 g / ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

### Roasted Broccoli
- 340 g / ¾ lb broccoli crowns, cut into florets
- 14 g / 1 Tbsp neutral oil
- 1.5 g / ½ tsp salt
- 1 g / ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

---

## Method

### 1. Start the potatoes and oven
Put the potatoes in a pot with 2 L / 8 cups cold water and 19 g / 2 Tbsp salt. Bring to a boil and cook 15 to 20 minutes, until a knife slides in with no resistance. Meanwhile, set racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven, put an empty sheet pan on the upper rack, and preheat to 450°F / 230°C.

### 2. Season the chicken
Pat the thighs dry. Mix the 9 g / 1 Tbsp lemon pepper with the 3 g / 1 tsp salt and season both sides of the chicken. The blend already contains salt, so this stays lighter than a normal dry brine. Let the chicken sit at room temperature while the potatoes cook.

### 3. Smash and roast the potatoes
Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for 2 minutes. Coat a second sheet pan with the 28 g / 2 Tbsp oil. Spread the potatoes on it and press each one flat to about ½ inch with the bottom of a measuring cup. Turn each one to coat it in oil, then season with the salt and pepper. Roast on the lower rack for 25 to 30 minutes, until deeply browned and crisp at the edges. You don't need to flip them.

### 4. Roast the broccoli
When the potatoes have 15 minutes left, toss the florets with the oil, salt, and pepper. Spread them on the preheated pan on the upper rack in a single layer and roast 12 to 15 minutes, until charred at the edges.

### 5. Sear the chicken
Once the broccoli goes in, heat the 14 g / 1 Tbsp oil in a carbon steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Lay in the thighs smooth side down and cook undisturbed 5 to 6 minutes, until well browned. Flip and cook 4 to 5 minutes more, to 175°F / 80°C internal. Move the chicken to a plate.

### 6. Make the lemon pepper butter
Drop the heat to low and let the pan cool for 30 seconds. Add the butter and the 3 g / 1 tsp lemon pepper. Swirl for 30 seconds until the butter foams and smells toasty, scraping up the fond. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the lemon juice, and return the chicken and any juices, turning to coat. Don't add any more salt: the butter is salted and the blend has salt in it.

### 7. Serve
Plate the chicken with the potatoes and broccoli, and spoon the leftover lemon pepper butter from the pan over the potatoes.
