# Oven-Baked Ground Beef Kebabs

_Adapted from the Easy Oven Baked Ground Beef Kebabs recipe on Explore Cook Eat._

## Ingredients

### Kebabs
- 454 g / 1 lb 80/20 ground chuck
- 60 g / ¼ cup grated yellow onion, squeezed dry in a towel
- 10 g / ¼ cup finely chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 6 g / 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 5 g / 1 Tbsp Aleppo pepper (Burlap & Barrel Aleppo-Style Pepper)
- 2 g / 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 g / 1 tsp ground coriander
- 2 g / 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 4 g / 1½ tsp salt

### Roasted Vegetables
- 170 g / 2 Roma tomatoes, each cut into 4 wedges
- 50 g / 2 jalapeños, halved lengthwise

### Garlic Yogurt Sauce
- 120 g / ½ cup Fage Total 5% Greek yogurt
- 3 g / 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 10 ml / 2 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 3 g / 1 Tbsp finely chopped fresh mint
- 1 g / ½ tsp sumac
- 0.5 g / ⅛ tsp salt

### To Serve
- 120 g / 2 rounds Kontos Greek pita, warmed

---

## Method

### 1. Prep the oven and skewers
Set a rack in the upper third of the oven and preheat to 425°F / 220°C. Line a rimmed sheet pan with foil and set a wire rack on top. Put 4 flat metal skewers in the fridge to chill.

### 2. Make the yogurt sauce
Stir together the yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, mint, sumac, and salt. Refrigerate until serving.

### 3. Mix the meat
In a large bowl, combine the onion, parsley, garlic, Aleppo pepper, cumin, coriander, black pepper, and salt. Add the beef and knead by hand for about 2 minutes, until the mixture turns tacky and sticks to your hand. If you have time, chill it for 10 minutes while the oven heats.

### 4. Shape the kebabs
Divide the meat into 4 portions of about 115 g each. With wet hands, press each portion firmly onto a chilled skewer in an even log about 7 inches long. Press light ridges along the length with your fingers.

### 5. Roast
Set the kebabs, tomato wedges, and jalapeño halves on the rack. Roast 10 minutes, flip the kebabs, and roast 7 minutes more.

### 6. Broil
Switch the oven to broil and cook 2 to 3 minutes, watching closely, until the tops are browned and charred in spots. The kebabs are done at 160°F / 71°C internal.

### 7. Serve
Rest the kebabs 2 minutes. Serve with the roasted tomatoes and jalapeños, warm pita, and the garlic yogurt sauce.
