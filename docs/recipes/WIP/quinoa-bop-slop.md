# Quinoa Bibimbap Meal Prep Bowls
_Adapted from a bibimbap bowl, reworked into weekly meal prep with quinoa in place of rice and Bibigo Korean BBQ marinade for the beef._

## Ingredients

### Quinoa
- 340g (2 cups) white quinoa, rinsed
- 15ml (1 tbsp) neutral oil, for toasting
- 680ml (2¾ cups) chicken or beef stock
- 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt

### Beef
- 680g (1.5 lb) thin-sliced beef (ribeye or sirloin style)
- 240ml (1 cup) Bibigo Korean BBQ marinade
- 15ml (1 tbsp) neutral oil, for searing

### Spinach Gomae
- 340g (12 oz) baby spinach
- 15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil
- 15ml (1 tbsp) Kikkoman soy sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 clove garlic, grated

### Bean Sprouts (Kongnamul)
- 340g (12 oz) fresh bean sprouts
- 10ml (2 tsp) toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1 scallion, thinly sliced

### Carrot
- 3 large carrots, julienned (~300g)
- 10ml (2 tsp) neutral oil
- 1/4 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt

### Mushroom
- 227g (8 oz) shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced
- 15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil
- 15ml (1 tbsp) Kikkoman soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp sugar

### Gochujang Sauce
- 60g (3 tbsp) Chung Jung One gochujang
- 15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil
- 15ml (1 tbsp) Marukan rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 clove garlic, grated

### Daily (not batched)
- 1 egg per bowl, fried, crispy edges/runny yolk

---

## Method

### 1. Toast and Cook the Quinoa
Heat the neutral oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the rinsed and dried quinoa and toast, stirring, for 2-3 minutes until fragrant and lightly golden. Add the stock and salt, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook 15 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Fluff and let cool before portioning.

### 2. Marinate and Sear the Beef
Toss the thin-sliced beef with the Bibigo marinade and let sit at least 30 minutes (up to a few hours in the fridge). Heat oil in a pan or on a griddle over high heat. Sear the beef in batches, not crowding the pan, 1-2 minutes per side until charred at the edges. Set aside to cool before portioning.

### 3. Blanch the Spinach
Bring a pot of water to a boil. Blanch spinach 30 seconds, then shock in cold water. Squeeze out as much liquid as possible. Toss with sesame oil, soy sauce, sesame seeds, and grated garlic.

### 4. Blanch the Bean Sprouts
In the same pot, blanch bean sprouts 2 minutes until just tender. Drain and shock briefly in cold water. Toss with sesame oil, salt, and scallion.

### 5. Sauté the Carrot
Heat neutral oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add julienned carrot and salt, sauté 2-3 minutes until just softened but still with bite.

### 6. Sauté the Mushroom
In the same pan, sauté shiitake in sesame oil until browned, about 4 minutes. Add soy sauce and sugar, toss to coat, cook 1 more minute.

### 7. Make the Gochujang Sauce
Whisk together gochujang, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar, and grated garlic until smooth. Store in a squeeze bottle or jar.

### 8. Serve
Portion quinoa, beef, and each namul into containers for the week. Each morning, fry an egg to your liking and add fresh on top along with a drizzle of gochujang sauce just before eating.
