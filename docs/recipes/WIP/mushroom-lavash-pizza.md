# Mushroom Lavash Pizza with Quick Tomato Sauce

_WIP — loosely based on Atoria's Mushroom Lavash Pizza with Quick Tomato Sauce, made with Atoria's mini lavash breads. Baking parameters are estimated from Atoria's other published lavash pizza recipes (their site was unreachable to pull the exact numbers) — treat times/temp as a starting point and adjust after the first bake._

## Ingredients

### Quick Tomato Sauce
- 400g (14 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 15ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Salt, to taste

### Mushrooms
- 227g (8 oz) cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 15g (1 tbsp) butter
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

### Assembly
- 4-6 Atoria's mini lavash breads
- 170g (6 oz) mozzarella, shredded
- 25g (1/4 cup) Parmesan, grated
- Fresh thyme or parsley, for finishing

---

## Method

### 1. Make the Quick Tomato Sauce
Heat the olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the crushed tomatoes and oregano, bring to a simmer, and cook 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened. Season with salt to taste — go easy at first, then adjust once the sauce has reduced.

### 2. Sauté the Mushrooms
Melt the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and any released liquid has evaporated, 6-8 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds more. Season with salt and pepper — since the butter is salted, hold back on added salt until the end and taste before adjusting.

### 3. Assemble
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the center. If baking directly on a sheet pan, no need to preheat it first — the lavash is already fully baked and just needs to crisp and heat through.
2. Arrange the mini lavash breads on one or two sheet pans.
3. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce on each, leaving a small border at the edges.
4. Top with shredded mozzarella, then the sautéed mushrooms, then the Parmesan.

### 4. Bake
Bake for 6-9 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the edges of the lavash are crisp and lightly browned. Watch closely toward the end — mini lavash is thin and can go from crisp to overly dark quickly.

### 5. Serve
Finish with fresh thyme or parsley. Slice and serve immediately.
