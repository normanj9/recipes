# Cornbread Loaf
_Adapted from the Skillet Cornbread recipe, converted for a 9x5-inch loaf pan._

## Ingredients

### Dry
- 160g / 1 cup medium-grind cornmeal (Bob's Red Mill Medium Grind Yellow)
- 120g / 1 cup all-purpose flour (King Arthur)
- 6g / 1 tsp baking powder
- 3g / ½ tsp baking soda
- 6g / 1 tsp salt
- 12g / 1 tbsp sugar _(optional — skip entirely for fully savory)_

### Wet
- 2 large eggs
- 240g / 1 cup buttermilk _(no buttermilk? Add 1 tbsp white vinegar to 1 cup whole milk and let sit 5 min)_
- 57g / 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted, plus extra for greasing the pan

### Topping (half the loaf only)
- 2–3 fresh jalapeños, sliced into thin rounds

---

## Method

### 1. Preheat and Prep the Pan
Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan well with butter, and line with parchment paper leaving an overhang on the long sides for easy removal.

### 2. Mix Dry
Whisk together the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar (if using) in a large bowl.

### 3. Mix Wet
Whisk eggs, buttermilk, and melted butter together in a separate bowl or large measuring cup.

### 4. Combine
Pour wet into dry and fold until just combined — stop when you no longer see dry flour. A few lumps are fine. Overmixing makes it tough.

### 5. Bake
Pour batter into the prepared pan and spread level. Arrange jalapeño rounds over one half lengthwise. Bake 45–55 minutes, until the top is deep golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Tent loosely with foil after 30 minutes if the top is browning too fast.

### 6. Serve
Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then lift out using the parchment overhang. Rest another 10 minutes on a rack before slicing.
