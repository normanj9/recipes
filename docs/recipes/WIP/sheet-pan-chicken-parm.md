# Sheet Pan Chicken Parm

_Adapted from the Sheet Pan Chicken Parm recipe on Sohla El-Waylly's TikTok._

## Ingredients

### Sauce Base
- 680–910 g / 1 (24–32 oz) jar marinara

### Chicken
- 48 g / 3 tbsp double-concentrated tomato paste
- 4 g / 1½ tbsp Italian seasoning
- 3 g / 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 g / ½ tsp chili flakes
- 27 g / 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 3 g / scant 1 tsp salt
- 565–680 g / 1¼–1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs

### Topping
- 60 g / ½ cup seasoned Italian breadcrumbs
- 45 g / ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 170–225 g / 1½–2 cups grated part-skim mozzarella

### To Serve
- Simple green salad
- Crusty bread

---

## Method

### 1. Set up the broiler
Set a rack about 8 inches from the broiler element and heat the broiler to high. Pour the marinara across a sheet pan.

### 2. Prep and coat the chicken
Trim any large pockets of fat from the thighs, then open each one up flat. If any are much thicker than the rest, cover with plastic wrap and pound to an even ½ inch so they cook at the same rate. In a large bowl, stir together the tomato paste, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, chili flakes, olive oil, and salt. Add the thighs and massage the paste evenly over them with your hands. If you have time, marinate overnight. The marinara, breadcrumbs, and Parmesan are all salty, so don't add salt anywhere else.

### 3. Broil the chicken
Arrange the thighs smooth side up on top of the marinara. Broil, rotating the pan and moving thighs around as needed, until charred and cooked through, 12 to 16 minutes. Thighs are forgiving, so err on the side of more time; they're at their best around 175°F, well past the point where breasts would dry out. If the marinara at the edges of the pan starts to scorch, stir it in toward the center. Meanwhile, stir together the breadcrumbs and Parmesan.

### 4. Top and finish
Sprinkle the mozzarella over everything, then scatter the crumbs on top, keeping most of them on the thighs. Gently press the crumbs onto the thighs and drizzle generously with olive oil. Broil, rotating the pan as needed, until golden and crisp, 2 to 4 minutes.

### 5. Serve
Serve right away with a simple green salad and crusty bread alongside.
