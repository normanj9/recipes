# Camarones a la Diabla

_Original recipe, built on [Chile Rojo Base](../sauces/chile-rojo-base.md). Serves 2._

## Ingredients

### Shrimp

- 1 lb (450g) large shrimp (16–20 count), peeled and deveined, tails on or off
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

### Diabla Sauce

- 6 cubes (3 squares) Chile Rojo Base, thawed — about 3 tbsp / 48g
- 4–6 dried chiles de árbol, stemmed (4 = hot, 6 = properly devilish)
- 1 chipotle in adobo, plus 1 tsp adobo sauce
- 1 Roma tomato (~100g), roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup (120 mL) chicken stock
- 1/4 white onion (~40g), finely diced
- 1 tbsp butter

### To Finish

- 1/2 lime, juiced, plus wedges to serve
- Small handful cilantro, chopped
- Salt, to taste

---

## Method

### 1. Season Shrimp

1. Pat shrimp dry and toss with 1/2 tsp salt. Set aside while you make the sauce.

### 2. Blend Sauce

1. Toast chiles de árbol in a dry skillet over medium heat, turning constantly, 30–60 seconds until fragrant and a shade darker. They burn fast — pull them the moment they smell toasty.
2. Heat the stock until steaming (microwave is fine) and soak the toasted árbol in it 10 minutes.
3. Blend árbol and stock, Chile Rojo Base, chipotle, adobo sauce, and tomato until completely smooth.
4. Don't salt yet — the base, the stock, and the butter all bring salt.

### 3. Sear Shrimp

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
2. Add shrimp in a single layer and sear 1 minute without moving, until pink on the bottom. Flip and cook 30 seconds more — they should still be translucent in the center.
3. Transfer to a plate.

### 4. Fry the Sauce

1. Lower heat to medium. Add onion to the pan and cook 2–3 minutes until softened and translucent.
2. Pour in the blended sauce (it will spatter). Fry, stirring, 2 minutes until it darkens a shade.
3. Simmer 3–5 minutes, stirring, until thick enough to coat a spoon heavily. Splash in water if it tightens up too much.

### 5. Finish

1. Reduce heat to low and swirl in butter until glossy.
2. Return shrimp and any juices to the pan. Toss 1–2 minutes until just opaque through.
3. Off heat, squeeze in lime juice. Taste and adjust salt — it likely needs little or none.
4. Top with cilantro and serve immediately with lime wedges, [Arroz Rojo](arroz-rojo.md) or white rice, and warm tortillas.
