# Mini Lavash Pizzas

_WIP — baking parameters adapted from Atoria's Family Bakery's [Mushroom Lavash Pizza with Quick Tomato Sauce](https://atoriasfamilybakery.com/blogs/recipes/mushroom-lavash-pizza-with-quick-tomato-sauce); scaled for Atoria's mini lavash. Toppings/quantities still TBD._

## Ingredients

- Atoria's mini lavash breads
- Tomato sauce, a thin layer per lavash
- Mozzarella cheese, shredded
- Mushrooms, sliced
- Garlic, minced
- Olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

---

## Method

### 1. Preheat
Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

### 2. Cook the Mushrooms
Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until tender, 3–4 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cook 1 more minute. Season with salt and pepper off heat — go light on the salt since the mushrooms are just a topping accent here.

### 3. Assemble
Arrange the mini lavash on the baking sheet. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce on each — mini lavash are smaller and thinner than the standard wraps this method was written for, so they'll burn before the center cooks through if oversauced or overtopped. Top with the mushrooms and a light sprinkle of mozzarella.

### 4. Bake
Bake for 6–8 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the edges are crisp. Since these are mini, start checking at the 5-minute mark — they likely need less time than full-size lavash.

### 5. Serve
Let cool a minute or two, then serve.
