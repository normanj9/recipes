# Chipotle Chicken Bacon Ranch Lavash Wraps

_An original meal-prep recipe. The chicken marinade is built on the [Chile Rojo Base](../sauces/chile-rojo-base.md), with chipotle and Mama Lil's peppers added. Makes 5 lunches._

## Ingredients

### Chipotle Chile Rojo Marinade
- 60g (1/4 cup) [Chile Rojo Base](../sauces/chile-rojo-base.md), thawed (about 4 of the 2x2 squares)
- 3 chipotles in adobo, finely minced, plus 15ml (1 tbsp) adobo sauce from the can
- 30ml (2 tbsp) oil from the Mama Lil's jar
- 30ml (2 tbsp) apple cider vinegar
- 12g (1 tbsp) brown sugar
- 2g (1 tsp) ground cumin
- 7g (2½ tsp) salt

### Chicken
- 910g (2 lb) boneless, skinless chicken thighs

### Bacon
- 340g (12 oz) bacon, about 10–12 strips

### Chipotle Ranch
- 150ml (2/3 cup) ranch dressing
- 15ml (1 tbsp) adobo sauce from the chipotle can

### Wraps
- 5 Atoria's lavash breads
- 140g (about 1¼ cups) shredded pepper jack or Mexican-blend cheese
- 150g (about 3 cups) shredded romaine hearts or iceberg
- 70g (about 1/3 cup) Mama Lil's peppers, drained and roughly chopped
- Pickled red onion (optional, see [Taco Salads](../meal-prep/taco-salads.md#1-pickle-the-onion) for the method)

---

## Method

### 1. Marinate the Chicken
Whisk all the marinade ingredients together in a large bowl or zip-top bag. Trim any big pockets of fat from the thighs. If any are much thicker than the rest, pound them to an even ½ inch so they cook evenly and slice into neat strips later. Add the thighs and massage the marinade in to coat. Marinate at least 4 hours or overnight (up to 24 hours).

The salt is set a bit under what the chicken would need on its own (about 0.8% of the meat's weight) because the bacon, cheese, and ranch all bring a lot of salt to the wrap. The base itself is only lightly salted, so it barely counts.

### 2. Bake the Chicken and Bacon
Set racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven and heat to 425°F. Line two sheet pans with foil.

Lay the thighs smooth side up on one pan, spaced so they roast instead of steam. Scrape any marinade left in the bag over the tops. Lay the bacon out in a single layer on the second pan.

Bake both pans together, chicken on the upper rack. Start checking the bacon at 15 minutes and pull it when it's crisp, 15–20 minutes total. Drain it on paper towels. The chicken takes 20–25 minutes, until it reads about 175°F in the thickest part. The sugar and chile paste will char at the edges, and that's what you want. If you want more char, broil 2–3 minutes at the end, but watch closely, because the paste goes from charred to burnt fast.

### 3. Rest and Slice
Let the chicken rest 10 minutes, then slice across the grain into ¼-inch strips. Spoon any pan juices over the slices. Let the chicken and bacon cool completely before building the wraps, because warm fillings steam the lavash and make it soggy.

### 4. Make the Chipotle Ranch
Stir the adobo sauce into the ranch. To make it hotter, add half a minced chipotle.

### 5. Build the Wraps
Lavash cracks when it's cold, so let it come to room temperature first. If it still feels stiff, give each sheet about 10 seconds in the microwave.

For each wrap, lay a lavash with a short side toward you and work in the bottom third:

1. Sprinkle a line of cheese first. It sits against the bread and acts as a moisture barrier.
2. Add a line of chicken (about 1/5 of the batch) and 2 strips of bacon, broken to fit.
3. Spoon about 2 tbsp of chipotle ranch in a stripe down the middle of the filling. Don't spread it to the edges of the bread.
4. Top with Mama Lil's peppers, pickled onion if using, and lettuce last, on the inside of the roll.
5. Roll up tightly from the short side, tucking the filling in on the first turn.

Wrap each one tightly in plastic wrap or foil and refrigerate seam-side down. Cut them in half on the bias when you eat them.

### 6. Storage
Eat within 4 days. For a fifth day, keep that day's chicken, bacon, and ranch in a container and roll the wrap that morning. It only takes 2 minutes. For the crispest wraps, you can keep the ranch on the side for dipping instead of rolling it in.

## Notes
- **Tomato:** skipped on purpose. It's the wettest thing you could put in a lavash wrap that sits for days, and cold tomato gets mealy. The Mama Lil's peppers and pickled onion already cover the sweet-tangy role. If you do want tomato, use seeded, diced Roma and keep it in its own container to add day-of.
- **Heat:** the guajillo/pasilla base is mild. Most of the heat comes from the chipotles, the Mama Lil's oil, and the peppers themselves. For a milder version, cut the chipotles to 2 and swap the Mama Lil's oil for olive oil.
- **Scaling:** this is 1 full-size lavash per lunch with about 4 oz of cooked chicken in each. If you're using Atoria's mini lavash, plan on 2 per lunch.
- Not yet tested. Confirm the bake times, the salt level, and how well the wraps hold up by day 4.
