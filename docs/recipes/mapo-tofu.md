# Mapo Tofu

_Adapted from Kenji's recipe on Serious Eats._

## Ingredients

### For the Wok
- Vegetable oil: 1/4 cup
- Ground beef: 1/4 pound

### Aromatics
_Combine in a small bowl._
- Garlic, grated on a microplane: 3 cloves
- Fresh ginger, grated on a microplane: 1 tablespoon

### Braising Sauce
_Combine in a small bowl or measuring cup._
- Fermented chili bean paste (doubanjiang): 2 tablespoons
- Amontillado sherry (sub Shaoxing wine): 2 tablespoons
- Dark soy sauce: 1 tablespoon
- Chicken stock: 1/4 cup

### Cornstarch Slurry
_Combine in a small bowl._
- Cornstarch: 1 teaspoon
- Cold water: 2 teaspoons

### Tofu
- Medium to firm silken tofu, cut into 1/2-inch cubes: 1½ pounds

### To Finish
_Combine in a small bowl — half the scallions only._
- Roasted chili oil (already Sichuan-peppercorn infused — no need to make your own): 1/4 cup
- Scallion greens, finely sliced, divided in half: 1/4 cup

### Garnish
_Combine on a small plate._
- Remaining scallion greens
- Toasted ground Sichuan peppercorn (pre-made — no toasting/grinding needed)

---

## Method

### 1. Prep
1. Combine the cornstarch slurry ingredients in a small bowl, mixing with a fork until homogeneous.
2. Bring a medium saucepan of water to a boil over high heat and add the tofu. Cook for 1 minute, then drain in a colander, being careful not to break up the tofu.

### 2. Cook the Beef
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a wok over high heat until smoking.
2. Add the beef and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.

### 3. Build the Sauce
1. Add the aromatics and cook until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
2. Add the braising sauce and bring to a boil.
3. Pour in the cornstarch slurry and cook for 30 seconds until thickened.

### 4. Add the Tofu and Finish
1. Add the tofu and carefully fold in, being careful not to break it up too much.
2. Stir in the chili oil and reserved half of scallions and simmer for 30 seconds longer.

### 5. Serve
1. Transfer immediately to a serving bowl and top with the garnish. Serve immediately with white rice.
