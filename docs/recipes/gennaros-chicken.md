# Gennaro's Chicken

_Adapted from citalia.com._

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- Chicken thighs, bone-in and skin-on: **4 pieces**
- Olive oil: **2 tbsp**
- Jalapeno: **1, whole**
- Garlic: **1 head, peeled**
- Fresh rosemary sprigs: **2–3**

### Sauce
- Dry white wine: **100 ml**
- Chicken stock: **100 ml**

---

## Method

### 1. Cold-Start Chicken
1. Season the chicken thighs generously with salt and black pepper.  
2. Put olive oil in dutch oven and add chicken, skin side down. Turn heat to medium high and cook until chicken releases and skin is golden brown.

### 2. Add Remaining Ingredients
1. Pour in the white wine and let it bubble for 1–2 minutes.  
2. Add the chicken stock.  
3. Add the garlic cloves, rosemary, and jalapeno.  
4. Cover the pan and cook gently for **30 minutes**.

### 3. Serve
1. Transfer chicken to plates, spoon sauce and garlic over the top, and serve hot with bruschetta.
