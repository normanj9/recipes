    Malasadas¶

    Adapted from the Malasadas recipe on Hummingbird High.

    Yield: 6 donuts
    Prep: 10 min + overnight rise
    Cook: ~4 min per donut

    Ingredients¶

    Dough¶

    • Bread flour: 2 cups (255 g)
    • Granulated sugar: 1 tsp (4 g)
    • Instant yeast: 1 packet (0.25 oz / 7 g)
    • Kosher salt: 1/2 tsp (3 g)
    • Whole milk, warmed to 120–130°F: 3/4 cup (170 g)
    • Large egg: 1
    • Large egg yolk: 1
    • Unsalted butter, melted: 2 tbsp (28 g)
    • Extra flour for dusting

    Passion Fruit Pastry Cream¶

    • Granulated sugar: 6 tbsp (75 g) total (divided)
    • Whole milk: 1 cup (227 g)
    • Large egg: 1
    • Cornstarch: 2 tbsp (14 g)
    • Kosher salt: 1/4 tsp
    • Unsalted butter: 1 tbsp (14 g)
    • Passion fruit purée: 1/4 cup (57 g)
    • Vanilla extract: 1/4 tsp

    For Frying & Coating¶

    • Neutral oil for frying
    • Granulated sugar: 3/4 cup (149 g) (for coating)

    Method¶

    1. Make the Dough (Day 1)¶

    1. In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, yeast, and salt. Make a small well in the center.
    2. Whisk together the warm milk, egg, egg yolk, and melted butter. Pour into the dry ingredients.
    3. Mix and knead (stand mixer or by hand) until smooth and elastic. Dough will be sticky.
    4. Cover bowl and refrigerate overnight.

    2. Make the Pastry Cream (Day 1)¶

    1. Heat 1/4 cup of the sugar with the milk in a saucepan until steaming.
    2. In a bowl, whisk remaining sugar with egg and cornstarch until smooth.
    3. Temper egg mixture with some hot milk, then return everything to the saucepan.
    4. Cook, whisking constantly, until thick and bubbling.
    5. Remove from heat; whisk in butter, passion fruit purée, and vanilla.
    6. Strain, cover with plastic wrap pressed to the surface, and chill overnight.

    3. Shape (Day 2)¶

    1. Lightly flour the counter. Roll dough to 1/2 inch thick.
    2. Cut 6 rounds with a 3-inch cutter. Re-roll scraps if needed.
    3. Place on a sheet pan, spaced apart. Cover and rise 1 hour.

    4. Fry¶

    1. Heat oil to 365°F in a heavy pot.
    2. Fry 2 donuts at a time, about 2 minutes per side, until golden.
    3. Drain on a rack briefly, then toss warm donuts in sugar.

    5. Fill¶

    1. Let donuts cool about 15 minutes.
    2. Use a skewer or pastry tip to open a cavity.
    3. Pipe in ~2 tbsp pastry cream per donut.

    6. Serve¶

    • Best eaten same day. Store loosely covered for up to 24 hours.
